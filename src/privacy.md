  <p class="legal-updated">Last updated: 2026-08-05</p>

Open Decks Worldwide is built to collect as little personal information
as possible. This page explains what little there is.

## No accounts, no tracking of visitors

There are no user accounts, logins, or profiles on this Site, and we do
not run any analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts. We don't know
who you are when you browse the Site, and we don't want to.

The one exception: your browser's light/dark theme choice is saved
locally on your own device using `localStorage`. It never leaves your
browser and is never sent to us.

If you use the "📍 Near me" feature, your location is requested directly
by your browser's built-in geolocation API and used only to sort events
by distance, entirely on your device — it's never transmitted to or
stored by us.

## Google Search Console

We use [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console/about)
to monitor how the Site appears in Google Search (e.g., indexing status,
search queries that lead to the Site, crawl errors). This is aggregated,
anonymized search-performance data provided by Google — it does not tell
us who any individual visitor is. Google's own privacy practices apply to
how they collect and process this data; see
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Hosting

The Site is a static website hosted on Microsoft Azure (Azure Static Web
Apps), served from United States datacenters. Standard web server logs
(e.g., IP address, request time, requested page) may be collected briefly
by Microsoft's hosting infrastructure for security and operational
purposes, under [Microsoft's Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/en-us/privacystatement).
We do not access, process, or retain these logs ourselves.

## Event and venue submissions

If you submit a venue or event:

- **Via GitHub pull request** — your GitHub username and submission are
  public as part of GitHub's platform, per
  [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).
- **Via email** — we receive your email address and message as sent. We
  use this only to verify and, if needed, follow up about the submission.
  **We do not publish your email address, name, or any other sender
  details alongside the published listing** — only the venue/event
  information itself goes live. Submission emails are not sold, shared
  with third parties, or used for any purpose beyond verifying and
  publishing that specific submission.

## Your rights (GDPR)

If you're in the EU/EEA (or anywhere with similar data protection law),
you have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you (which, in
practice, is at most a submission email you sent us), and to request its
correction or deletion. Our legal basis for briefly processing a
submission email is our legitimate interest in verifying and publishing
accurate event listings. To make a request, email
[submit@opendecks.xyz](mailto:submit@opendecks.xyz).

## Venue and event data isn't "your" data

Published listings (venue names, addresses, event times, links, etc.)
describe public businesses and public events, not private individuals.
If you're a venue owner or event organizer and want a listing corrected
or removed, email [submit@opendecks.xyz](mailto:submit@opendecks.xyz) and
we'll handle it promptly.

## Changes

This policy may be updated as the project evolves. Material changes will
be reflected here with an updated date.

## Contact
