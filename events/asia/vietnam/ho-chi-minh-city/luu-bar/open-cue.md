Weekly open decks night at Luu Bar. Sets are 1-hour slots, reduced to 30
minutes if 3 or more DJs are waiting to play. Follow the flow of the previous
DJ, play for the crowd, and keep the energy moving.

Music lovers and friends, come through to support the selectors, grab a
drink, and shape the vibe.
