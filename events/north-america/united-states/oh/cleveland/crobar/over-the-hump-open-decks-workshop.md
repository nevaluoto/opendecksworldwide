Whether you're curious about DJing, taking your first steps behind the
decks, refining your skills, or simply looking to connect with other music
lovers, everyone is welcome. Volunteer mentors are available throughout the
night to answer questions, offer guidance, and help you explore professional
club equipment.

Bring your laptop, USBs, or gear for hands-on assistance with Rekordbox,
Serato, music organization, workflow tips, and general DJ support. Each
month also features a guest speaker from the Cleveland DJ community sharing
their journey, insights, and advice for developing artists — the August 13
session features Navy Jayde.
